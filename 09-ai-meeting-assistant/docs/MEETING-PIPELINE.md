# Meeting intelligence pipeline

1. Accept audio or transcript uploads through an authenticated endpoint.
2. Scan and validate files; transcribe audio using an approved speech-to-text service.
3. Identify speakers only where policy and consent permit; preserve timestamps.
4. Generate summary, decisions, risks and action items with evidence references.
5. Require human review before assigning tasks or updating project systems.
6. Apply retention, deletion and access policies to recordings and transcripts.

This starter accepts text notes only; transcription, diarization and calendar integrations are not configured.
