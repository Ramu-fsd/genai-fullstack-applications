# Financial AI guardrails

- Use governed, reconciled source data with explicit as-of timestamps and currency units.
- Separate deterministic calculations from model-generated narrative.
- Show formulas, source lineage, assumptions and confidence limitations.
- Do not fabricate missing figures or silently infer accounting definitions.
- Require qualified human review for investment, lending, risk or regulatory decisions.
- Enforce role-based access, audit trails and data-retention controls.

The included demo only formats supplied context; it does not connect to financial systems or provide financial advice.
