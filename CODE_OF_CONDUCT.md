# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in this repository respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish secrets or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation where necessary.

Report conduct concerns through the contact options on:
https://github.com/ramu-fsd
